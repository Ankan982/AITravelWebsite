Kashmir Trip Details

Kashmir | India

Kashmir Tour

“Kashmir is not just a destination—it’s a feeling stitched together with snow-kissed peaks, silent lakes, blooming gardens, and stories whispered by pine forests. From floating houseboats to gondola rides over snowfields, this journey invites you to slow down, breathe deeper, and fall in love with nature all over again.”

🛤️ Getting There

• Pickup & Drop: Srinagar Airport / Railway Station
• Well connected via major Indian cities, assisted pickup & drop included in the package

🌄 Why Choose Us

• Perfect balance of leisure, sightseeing & comfort.
• Stay in both hotels and a premium houseboat
• Covers Kashmir’s most iconic destinations—Srinagar, Sonamarg, Gulmarg & Pahalgam
• Ideal for families, couples & group travellers
• Flexible group pricing with private vehicles.


📆 Day-by-Day Itinerary


1️⃣ Day 1 – Arrival in Srinagar & Local Sightseeing

• Arrival at Srinagar Airport / Railway Station. Warm welcome and transfer to hotel.
• Later, enjoy Srinagar local sightseeing including Mughal Gardens, local markets, and lakeside views.
• Evening at leisure.
• Overnight Stay: Srinagar Hotel

2️⃣ Day 2 – Srinagar → Sonamarg → Srinagar

• After breakfast, proceed to Sonamarg, the “Meadow of Gold.”
• Enjoy snow-capped peaks, river streams, and optional pony rides (on direct payment)
• Return to Srinagar by evening
• Overnight Stay: Srinagar Hotel.


3️⃣ Day 3 – Srinagar → Gulmarg → Srinagar

• Post breakfast, drive to Gulmarg, the world-famous meadow and skiing destination.
• Enjoy scenic views, snow activities (seasonal), and optional Gondola cable car ride (direct payment).
• Return to Srinagar in the evening.
• Overnight Stay: Srinagar Hotel


4️⃣ Day 4 – Srinagar → Pahalgam Sightseeing & Stay

• Check out and drive to Pahalgam, the Valley of Shepherds
• Explore Betaab Valley, Aru Valley (as permitted), and Lidder River views.
• Relax in the serene environment of pine forests and flowing rivers.
• Overnight Stay: Pahalgam Hotel


5️⃣ Day 5 –Pahalgam → Srinagar | Houseboat Stay

• After breakfast, return to Srinagar
• Check into a super deluxe houseboat on Dal Lake.
• In the evening, enjoy a peaceful 1-hour Shikara Ride across floating gardens and local life on water.
• Overnight Stay: Srinagar Houseboat


6️⃣ Day 6 – Departure from Srinagar

• After breakfast, check out and transfer to Srinagar Airport / Railway Station for your onward journey.
• Tour Ends with Beautiful Memories.

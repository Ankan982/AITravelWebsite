📆 Day-by-Day Itinerary


1️⃣ Day 1 – Siliguri → Chatakpur (Eco Village Stay)

• Pickup from Siliguri.
• Scenic uphill drive through lush greenery and Sinchula forest.
• Arrive at Chatakpur Eco Village (7,800 ft).
• Evening nature walk through pine forests & bird-watching trails.
• Overnight stay in Chatakpur wooden homestay.

2️⃣ Day 2 – Chatakpur → Darjeeling Sightseeing
(Tranquil Trail begins)

• Early morning sunrise view of Kanchenjunga from Chatakpur viewpoint.
• Drive to Darjeeling.
• Full-day sightseeing: Tiger Hill, Batasia Loop, Ghoom Monastery, Himalayan Mountaineering Institute, Darjeeling Zoo, Tea Gardens, Mall Road & Chowrasta.
• Taste classic Darjeeling tea in heritage tea houses.
• Overnight stay in Darjeeling.


3️⃣ Day 3 – Darjeeling → Sukhiyapokhri → Lepchajagat

• Morning drive to Sukhiyapokhri, known for its peaceful monasteries and Indo-Nepal trade route vibes.
• Proceed to Lepchajagat (small hamlet at 6,956 ft, famous for silence & forests).
• Evening stroll into the woods, panoramic views of the hills.
• Overnight stay in Lepchajagat.


4️⃣ Day 4 –Lepchajagat → Tabakoshi (via Tea Gardens)

• Morning drive through valleys & orange orchards.
• Arrive at Tabakoshi, a picturesque riverside village.
• Sightseeing: Nitik village trails, tea garden walks, waterfalls, and river bridges.
• Local cultural interaction with villagers and tea garden workers.
• Overnight stay in Tabakoshi riverside cottage.


5️⃣ Day 5 –Tabakoshi → Mirik → Siliguri

• Morning drive to Mirik Lake.
• Leisure walk around the lake, option for boating and horse riding.
• Breakfast at Mirik with a lake view.
• Drive back to Siliguri for departure.


Food & Culinary Experiences of Darjeeling

Darjeeling is not just about tea—it’s a culinary blend of Nepali, Tibetan, Lepcha, and Bengali influences. On this trip, you’ll get to savor:
• Momo & Thukpa: Steamed dumplings and warm noodle soup—soul food for mountain evenings.
• Sel Roti & Aloo Dum: Traditional Nepali rice bread served with spicy potato curry.
• Darjeeling Tea: World-famous golden brew, best enjoyed in heritage tea houses.
• Gundruk Soup & Kinema Curry: Fermented mountain flavors unique to the Nepali-Tibetan culture.
• Tongba (Millet Beer): Optional, for those who want to sip tradition from bamboo mugs.
• Homemade Orange Wine (Tabakoshi Special): A sweet sip of the valley’s orchards.

  • Carry warm clothes—it gets chilly at night in the hills.
  • Try local food & tea—they are part of the culture.
  • Respect forest rules in eco zones like Chatakpur & Lepchajagat

🚫 Don’ts

  • Avoid littering in tea gardens and lakeside.
  • Do not expect luxury hotels in remote eco-villages—these are rustic homestays.

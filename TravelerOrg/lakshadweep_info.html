Lakshadweep Tour Details

Lakshadweep
“Where Every Shade of Blue Tells a Story.”

Imagine waking up to the sound of gentle waves, walking on powder-white beaches, exploring vibrant coral reefs, and witnessing endless shades of turquoise stretching to the horizon. Lakshadweep is not just another beach destination—it's India's most pristine tropical paradise. Our carefully curated 4 Nights / 5 Days Kavaratti & Agatti Tour offers the perfect blend of relaxation, adventure, and island life, making it an unforgettable escape for couples, families, honeymooners, and nature lovers.

🛤️ Getting There

Your journey begins with a flight to Agatti Airport (AGX). From Agatti, you'll travel to Kavaratti via an air-conditioned speedboat (approximately 2.5 hours), with all inter-island transfers included in the package.

🌄 Why Choose Us

• Natural Beauty: Crystal-clear turquoise lagoons, untouched white sand beaches, and beautiful coral reefs
• Adventure & Activities: Snorkelling, scuba diving, glass bottom boat ride, turtle watching, and Kalpitty Island excursion
• Lifestyle & Experience: Peaceful island lifestyle, ideal for honeymoon and family holidays
• Exclusivity: Limited tourist entry ensuring a private and exclusive experience


📆 Day-by-Day Itinerary


1️⃣ Day 1 – Welcome to Lakshadweep

• Arrival at Agatti Airport
• Welcome & Lunch
• AC Speedboat Transfer to Kavaratti
• Resort Check-in
• Evening Leisure beside the Lagoon
Overnight Stay: Kavaratti

2️⃣ Day 2 – Explore Kavaratti
After breakfast, enjoy the beauty of Kavaratti through exciting water activities and sightseeing.

• Water Activities: Snorkelling and kayaking experiences
• Cultural & Historic Spots: Lighthouse, local museum, and village tour.
• Scenic Locations: Eastern Jetty, island helipad, sunset point, and shipwreck site.
• Stay Details: Overnight stay at Kavaratti.


3️⃣ Day 3 – Kavaratti to Agatti
Morning speedboat transfer to Agatti.
Enjoy the peaceful beaches before heading out for sightseeing.

Places Covered :
• Agatti Village
• Archaeological Museum
• Jetties
• Lagoon Beach
Overnight Stay: Agatti


4️⃣ Day 4 – Adventure Day

• Adventure Activities: Snorkelling, kayaking, and scuba diving (all optional)
• Marine Experiences: Turtle watching and glass bottom boat ride
• Island Excursion: Kalpitty Island trip (subject to weather conditions)
Stay Details: Overnight stay at Agatti


5️⃣ Day 5 – Departure

• Transfer to Agatti Airport

• Return home with unforgettable island memories.

✅ Do’s

  • Clothing & Essentials: Light cotton clothing, sunscreen, sunglasses, hats, waterproof bags for electronics, and a valid government ID
  • Safety & Rules: Follow local administration guidelines, wear life jackets during water activities, and respect marine life and coral reefs
  • Environment Care: Keep beaches clean and practice eco‑friendly habits during your stay
  • Respect local culture and marine life

🚫 Don’ts

  • Environmental Care: Do not litter on beaches and avoid plastic waste
  • Marine Protection: Do not touch or collect corals, avoid feeding marine animals, and do not damage marine ecosystems
  • Local Regulations: Avoid alcohol consumption where prohibited and do not enter restricted island areas
  • Respect for Nature: Follow eco‑friendly practices to preserve marine life and habitats

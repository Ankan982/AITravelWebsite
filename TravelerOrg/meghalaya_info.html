Meghalaya Tour Details

Meghalaya – The Abode of Clouds | Meghalaya
“Where emerald hills kiss the clouds, waterfalls plunge from the heavens, and crystal-clear rivers mirror the sky — Meghalaya offers an unforgettable journey through nature's finest masterpiece. From the charming streets of Shillong to the breathtaking landscapes of Cherrapunjee and Dawki, every moment here feels like a page from a fairy tale.”

🌟 Tour Route

Guwahati → Shillong → Mawlynnong → Dawki → Cherrapunjee → Shillong → Guwahati


📆 Day-by-Day Itinerary


1️⃣ Day 1 – Guwahati – Shillong

• Arrival at Guwahati Airport/Railway Station.
• Drive towards the picturesque hill station of Shillong.
• Evening visit to Solomon’s Temple & Bara Bazar.
• En route visit the stunning Umiam Lake (Barapani).
• Explore Ward’s Lake, Cathedral of Mary Help of Christians, and Police Bazaar.
• Evening at leisure, Overnight Stay: Shillong

2️⃣ Day 2 – Shillong – Mawlynnong – Dawki – Shillong
(Trek Begins)

• Breakfast and proceed towards Mawlynnong, popularly known as Asia’s Cleanest Village
• Visit the famous Living Root Bridge at Riwai.
• Continue to Dawki and enjoy boating on the crystal-clear Umngot River.
• Visit the Indo-Bangladesh Border Viewpoint.
• Return to Shillong by evening. Overnight Stay: Shillong


3️⃣ Day 3 – Shillong – Cherrapunjee (Sohra)

• Check out and drive to Cherrapunjee.
• Visit the majestic Nohkalikai Falls, India's tallest plunge waterfall.
• Explore the fascinating Mawsmai Cave.
• Visit Seven Sisters Falls and Eco Park.
• Enjoy mesmerizing views of the Khasi Hills.
• Overnight at Hmuifang Tourist Lodge. Overnight Stay: Cherrapunjee.


4️⃣ Day 4 – Cherrapunjee Sightseeing

• Visit the spectacular Wei Sawdong Falls
• Explore the legendary Dainthlen Falls.
• Visit the naturally formed Arwah Cave.
• Optional adventure trek to the famous Nongriat Double Decker Living Root Bridge (at own expense).
• Evening at leisure amidst the serene hills.
• Overnight Stay: Cherrapunjee


5️⃣ Day 5 – Cherrapunjee – Shillong

• Check out and drive back to Shillong.
• Visit Mawkdok Dympep Valley View Point.
• Explore the beautiful Elephant Falls
• Visit Shillong Peak, the highest point in Meghalaya.
• Free time for shopping and local exploration at Police Bazaar.Overnight Stay: Shillong


6️⃣ Day 6 – Shillong – Guwahati Drop

• After breakfast, check out from the hotel.
• Visit the sacred Kamakhya Temple (subject to departure timing).
• Transfer to Guwahati Airport/Railway Station for onward journey.
• Tour Ends with Wonderful Memories

✅ Do’s

  • Carry comfortable trekking shoes
  • Keep warm clothing handy throughout the year.
  • Carry valid government photo ID.
  • Respect local customs and traditions.

🚫 Don’ts

  • Do not litter at tourist sites. Do not consume alcohol in public places.
  • Avoid venturing into restricted areas.
  • Avoid trekking during heavy rainfall without local guidance.

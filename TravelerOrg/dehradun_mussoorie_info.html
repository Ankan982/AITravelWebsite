Dehradun, Mussoorie Trip Details

Dehradun, Mussoorie | Dehradun,Mussoorie, Uttarakhand

Dehradun, Mussoorie, Landour & Chakrata – Whispers of the Doon Valley

“A 4-Day Himalayan Retreat through Dehradun, Mussoorie, Landour & Chakrata”

The journey begins in the foothills of the Himalayas, where the Doon Valley cradles history, legends, and pine-scented winds. From the calm embrace of Dehradun to the misty ridges of Mussoorie, the colonial charm of Landour, and the serene cliffs of Chakrata—the four days are a tapestry of hills, rivers, forests, and timeless stillness. This isn’t just a trip, it is a gentle conversation with the mountains, where every dawn feels like poetry and every sunset a blessing.

🛤️ Getting There

• By Air: Jolly Grant Airport, Dehradun (25 km from city centre) with regular flights from Delhi, Mumbai, and major Indian cities.
• By Rail: Dehradun Railway Station is well-connected with Delhi, Lucknow, Kolkata and Mumbai.
• By Road: Overnight Volvo buses and taxis from Delhi (approx. 6–7 hours drive).

Our team will provide pick-up and drop services from Dehradun Railway Station, ISBT, or Jolly Grant Airport.

🌄 Why Choose this Journey?

• Experience the rich blend of culture, history, and Himalayan landscapes in just 4 days.
• Visit Dehradun’s colonial gems and temples, stroll through Mussoorie’s buzzing Mall Road, discover Landour’s serene colonial lanes, and unwind at Chakrata’s peaceful hilltops.
• Perfect mix of sightseeing, leisure, and hidden mountain hamlets.
• Stay in hand-picked comfortable hotels that blend local warmth with modern amenities.

📆 Day-by-Day Itinerary

1️⃣ Day 1 – Arrival at Dehradun & City Sightseeing

• Pickup from Railway Station/ISBT/Airport.
• Check into hotel, welcome drink, and rest.
• Sightseeing: Forest Research Institute (FRI), Robber’s Cave (Guchhupani), Tapkeshwar Temple, and Tibetan Monastery.
• Evening stroll at Paltan Bazaar for local shopping and food tasting.
• Overnight stay in Dehradun hotel.


2️⃣ Day 2 –Dehradun → Kimadi Gaon → Mussoorie

• Breakfast and scenic drive through Kimadi Gaon, a rustic hamlet surrounded by oak and pine forests.
• Arrive in Mussoorie, the “Queen of Hills.”
• Sightseeing: Kempty Falls, Company Garden, Gun Hill, and Camel’s Back Road.
• Evening leisure at Mall Road.
• Overnight stay in Mussoorie hotel.

3️⃣ Day 3 – Mussoorie → Landour → Chakrata

• Morning drive to Landour, the quaint British cantonment.
• Explore Char Dukan cafés, St. Paul’s Church, Landour Bakehouse, and Lal Tibba (highest point of Mussoorie).
• Later drive towards Chakrata, a quieter Himalayan jewel.
• Evening bonfire and rest in peaceful mountain surroundings.
• Overnight stay in Chakrata.


4️⃣ Day 4 – Chakrata Sightseeing & Return to Dehradun

• Visit Tiger Falls, Deoban Forest (for Himalayan views), and explore Chakrata town.
• Leisure walk among pine and deodar forests.
• Return journey to Dehradun by evening.
• Drop at Dehradun Railway Station/Airport/ISBT.
• Journey concludes with a heart full of mountain memories.

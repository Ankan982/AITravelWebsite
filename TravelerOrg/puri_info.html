Puri, Odisha

Puri Beach Tour | Odisha

Puri, Odisha - The Golden Beach Paradise

"Where spirituality meets the sea, and ancient temples watch over endless golden shores — Puri is not just a destination, it is a pilgrimage of the soul by the ocean"

🛤️ Getting There

Arrival Point: Bhubaneswar Airport (Biju Patnaik International Airport)

Journey: Bhubaneswar → Puri (approximately 60 km, 2 hours drive)

Best Time to Visit: October to March for pleasant weather and sea breeze

🌄 Why Choose Puri Package ?
• Experience the famous Jagannath Temple, one of the four sacred pilgrimage sites
• Relax on the pristine Puri Beach with golden sands
• Explore the unique Rath Yatra (Chariot Festival) traditions
• Discover water sports and beach activities
• Visit nearby Konark Sun Temple, a UNESCO World Heritage Site
• Perfect for families, couples, and spiritual seekers
• Budget-friendly beach getaway with cultural immersion

📆 Day-by-Day Itinerary


1️⃣ Day 1 – Arrival in Puri

• Arrive at Bhubaneswar Airport and transfer to Puri (60 km journey)
• Check-in at your beachfront hotel
• Evening visit to the famous Jagannath Temple
• Witness the evening aarti (prayer ceremony)
• Stroll along Puri Beach and enjoy the sunset
• Overnight Stay: Puri

2️⃣ Day 2 – Puri & Konark Exploration

• After breakfast, visit Konark Sun Temple (UNESCO World Heritage Site) - 35 km away
• Marvel at the intricate stone carvings and ancient architecture
• Explore the nearby Konark Beach
• Return to Puri in the afternoon
• Visit Swargadwar Beach and Baliharachandi Temple
• Evening leisure time at the beach
• Overnight Stay: Puri

3️⃣ Day 3 – Water Sports & Beach Activities

• Morning water sports activities: Jet Skiing, Banana Boat rides, Parasailing
• Visit Raghurajpur Artist Village to see traditional Odisha art forms
• Lunch at a local seafood restaurant
• Afternoon beach volleyball and swimming
• Visit Puri Marine Aquarium
• Shopping at local markets for handicrafts
• Evening bonfire on the beach (seasonal)
• Overnight Stay: Puri

4️⃣ Day 4 – Departure

• Morning visit to Chakra Tirtha Beach for a final swim
• Breakfast and some last-minute shopping
• Transfer to Bhubaneswar Airport for onward journey
• Depart with beautiful memories of the sacred beaches

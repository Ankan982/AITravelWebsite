Responsible Travel

A Promise to Mother Nature

We are all guests on this planet.

Why it matters

Mountains, forests, and rivers are not just destinations — they are living ecosystems. As responsible travellers, our small actions can protect nature.

Let's travel responsibly. Let's give back to nature.

✅ Basic Guidelines
💧 Save Water
  • – Avoid wasting water
  • – Do not wash clothes unnecessarily
🚫 No Littering
  • – Do not throw plastic or wrappers
  • – Carry your waste back
  • – No spitting (gutkha / pan masala)
🔇 Respect Silence
  • – Avoid loud music
  • – Use headphones
🔥 Avoid Barbecue in Nature
  • – Do not light open fires
  • – It harms ecosystems & wildlife
🌸 Respect Local Culture
  • – Ask before taking photos
  • – Support local businesses
🐾 Protect Wildlife
  • – Do not feed animals
  • – Do not disturb them
🚶 Stay on Trails
  • – Avoid off-route walking
♻️ Reduce Plastic
  • – Carry reusable bottles
🏔️ Travel Mindfully
  • – Take memories, not waste

🌎 Respect nature, and it will reward you forever.

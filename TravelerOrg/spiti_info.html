Spiti Valley Circuit Trip Details

Spiti Valley | Himachal Pradesh

Spiti Valley Circuit Trip

“Beyond the green hills and bustling towns lies Spiti—an ancient Himalayan desert where monasteries guard centuries of wisdom, rivers carve deep gorges, and the sky feels impossibly close. This journey is not about ticking destinations; it is about slowing down, breathing thin air, and surrendering to a land that humbles every traveler
Spiti doesn’t impress—it transforms”

🛤️ Getting There

• Pickup & Drop: Chandigarh
• Chandigarh is well connected by air, rail, and road from major Indian cities. Pickup and drop assistance can be arranged on request

🌄 Why Choose Us

• A complete circular route covering Shimla–Kinnaur–Spiti–Lahaul–Manali.
• Gradual altitude gain ensures better acclimatization.
• Ancient monasteries older than a thousand years
• Some of the highest motorable villages in the world
• Iconic alpine lake Chandratal and majestic Kunzum Pas.
• A perfect blend of adventure, culture, spirituality, and raw nature


📆 Day-by-Day Itinerary


1️⃣ Day 1 – Chandigarh to Shimla

• Chandigarh to Shimla
• Scenic uphill drive through lush greenery and Sinchula forest.
• Evening free for a relaxed stroll at Mall Road & The Ridge, enjoying cool mountain air and sunset hues.
• Overnight Stay: Shimla

2️⃣ Day 2 – Shimla to Sangla / Chitkul

• A scenic Himalayan drive through apple orchards, pine forests, and river valleys
• Witness the turquoise flow of the Baspa River and the serenity of Sangla Valley.
• Optional visit to Chitkul, the last inhabited village near the Indo-Tibet border.
•Overnight Stay: Sangla / Chitkul


3️⃣ Day 3 – Sangla to Kalpa to Tabo

• Wake up to panoramic views of the sacred Kinnaur Kailash range at Kalpa.
• Post breakfast, drive deeper into the trans-Himalayan region, entering Spiti Valley.
• Arrive at Tabo, home to one of the oldest Buddhist monasteries in the Himalayas.
• Overnight Stay: Tabo


4️⃣ Day 4 – Tabo to Dhankar to Kaza

• Explore Tabo Monastery and its ancient murals.
• Proceed to the dramatic Dhankar Monastery, perched high above the Spiti River gorge.
• Optional short hike to Dhankar Lake for breathtaking views.
• Arrive at Kaza, the heart of Spiti Valley.
• Overnight Stay: Kaza


5️⃣ Day 5 – Kaza | High-Altitude Village Exploration

• A full day exploring Spiti’s cultural and geographical wonders.
• Visit the iconic Key Monastery, the largest in Spiti.
• Continue to some of the world’s highest villages—Kibber, Komik, and viewpoints near Chicham Bridge, Asia’s highest suspension bridge.
• Overnight Stay: Kaza


6️⃣ Day 6 – Kaza to Chandratal Lake

•Drive through rugged landscapes towards the magical Chandratal Lake, a moon-shaped alpine lake surrounded by towering peaks.
• Evening at leisure under star-lit skies, embracing silence and solitude.
• Continue to some of the world’s highest villages—Kibber, Komik, and viewpoints near Chicham Bridge, Asia’s highest suspension bridge.
• Overnight Stay:Chandratal Camp


7️⃣ Day 7 – Chandratal to Manali (via Kunzum Pass)

• Cross the majestic Kunzum Pass, bidding farewell to Spiti.
• Descend into the greener valleys of Lahaul and reach Manali by evening.
• Overnight Stay: Manali



8️⃣ Day 8 – Manali to Chandigarh

• After breakfast, begin your return journey to Chandigarh, carrying memories of barren mountains, ancient chants, and timeless landscapes.
• Tour Ends.

✅ Do’s

  • Carry adequate warm clothing and sturdy footwear
  • Stay hydrated and acclimatize properly
  • Respect local customs, monasteries and traditions

🚫 Don’ts

  • Do not litter—Spiti is fragile and sacred
  • Avoid alcohol at high altitudes
  • Do not rush the journey, mountains demand patience

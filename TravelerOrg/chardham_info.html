Char Dham Yatra Details

Char Dham Yatra

Char Dham Yatra – A Sacred Journey Through the Abode of Gods

“Where rivers are prayers, mountains are temples, and every step is a hymn”

🛤️ Getting There

Your pilgrimage begins in New Delhi, the heart of India, where we welcome you warmly. From the bustling streets of the capital, your journey flows towards the Himalayas, where the Char Dhams — Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath, and Badrinath — await your devotion. Travel in comfort by 4-seater sedan, SUV, XUV, or Tempo Traveller, depending on your group size. Our professional drivers and guides ensure a safe, smooth, and soul-stirring ride.

📆 12-Day Pilgrimage Itinerary (Delhi to Delhi)


1️⃣ Day 1 – Delhi to Haridwar / Rishikesh 2️⃣ Day 2 – Haridwar / Rishikesh to Barkot

• Morning departure from Delhi
• Scenic drive to Haridwar/Rishikesh
• Evening Ganga Aarti at Har Ki Pauri or Triveni Ghat
• Dinner and overnight stay in hotel
• Drive through Mussoorie hill roads with views of the Shivaliks
• Stop at Kempty Falls (optional) & Arrive at Barkot, gateway to Yamunotri

3️⃣ Day 3 – Barkot to Yamunotri 4️⃣ Day 4 – Barkot to Uttarkashi

• Drive to Jankichatti and trek 5–6 km (or pony/doli options) to Yamunotri Temple
• Worship Goddess Yamuna, take a holy dip in Surya Kund & return to Barkot for overnight stay
• Drive along the Yamuna and Bhagirathi rivers & Reach Uttarkashi, known for Vishwanath Temple
• Evening leisure and overnight stay


5️⃣ Day 5– Uttarkashi to Gangotri 6️⃣ Day 6 – Uttarkashi to Guptkashi

• Drive to Gangotri through Harsil Valley
• Offer prayers to Goddess Ganga at Gangotri Temple
• Witness the origin of the Ganges surrounded by pine and deodar forests & return to Uttarkashi for the night
• Long but scenic drive along river valleys
• Evening at leisure & Overnight stay in hotel


7️⃣ Day 7 – Guptkashi to Kedarnath (Trek) 8️⃣ Day 8 – Kedarnath to Guptkashi

• Drive to Sonprayag, then shuttle to Gaurikund
• Trek 16 km to Kedarnath (pony, palki, or helicopter options available)
• Visit Lord Shiva’s Kedarnath Temple — one of the 12 Jyotirlingas
• Overnight stay at Kedarnath (basic accommodation near temple)
• Morning darshan at Kedarnath & trek back to Gaurikund and drive to Guptkashi
• Overnight in hotel


9️⃣ Day 9 – Har Ki Dun to Osla/Seema (Descent) 🔟Day 10 – Har Ki Dun to Osla/Seema (Descent)

• Drive via Joshimath, with stop at Narad Kund and Vishnuprayag
• Evening darshan at Badrinath Temple
• Overnight in hotel
• Morning rituals at Badrinath Temple
• Visit Mana Village- the last Indian village before Tibet
• Explore Vasudhara Falls and Vyas Gufa (if weather allows) & Overnight stay in Badrinath


1️⃣1️⃣ Day 11 – Badrinath to Rudraprayag / Srinagar 1️⃣2️⃣ Day 12 – Return to Delhi

• Scenic drive through confluences of Alaknanda
• Overnight in hotel
• Drive back to New Delhi & Drop-off at your chosen location

✅ Do’s

  • Carry warm clothing, raincoat, and comfortable shoes, stay hydrated and eat light during travel
  • Keep personal medicines and basic medical kit
  • Respect temple customs and dress modestly
  • Listen to your guide and follow the yatra schedule

🚫 Don’ts

  • Avoid alcohol or intoxicants during the pilgrimage
  • Don’t litter or damage temple premises
  • Don’t photograph inside temples where prohibited
  • Avoid overexertion — pace yourself, especially at high altitude

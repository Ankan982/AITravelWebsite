Kumaon Yatra Details

Har Ki Dun Trek | Uttarkashi, Uttarakhand

Kumaon Yatra – A Journey Through the Himalayan Jewels

“Where misty lakes, snow-clad peaks, and serene valleys tell timeless tales.”

🛤️ Introduction

The Kumaon Himalayas are a hymn of rivers, forests, and mountains blending into divine harmony. From the shimmering reflections of Naini Lake to the snow-capped grandeur of Panchachuli Peaks in Munsiyari, from the tranquil tea estates of Kausani to the heritage meadows of Ranikhet, and the soulful silence of Chaukori, this Yatra takes you on an unforgettable ride.

Every dawn you rise to the chant of Himalayan winds, and every night you sleep under a blanket of stars—this is Kumaon, a land where heaven leans close to earth.

📆 Day-by-Day Itinerary


1️⃣ Day 1 – Arrival in Nainital (Delhi → Nainital | 7 hrs by road)

• Pickup from Delhi/Kathgodam Railway Station.
• Check-in at lakeside hotel in Nainital.
• Evening: Boat ride in Naini Lake, visit Naina Devi Temple, stroll along Mall Road.
• Overnight stay in Nainital.

2️⃣ Day 2 – Nainital Local Exploration
(Trek Begins)

• Morning visit to Snow View Point (cable car ride).
• Day tour to Bhimtal, Sattal & Naukuchiatal – each lake with unique charm.
• Optional adventure: Kayaking, paragliding.
• Evening free for shopping at Tibetan Market.
• Overnight in Nainital.


3️⃣ Day 3 –Drive to Munsiyari (Nainital → Munsiyari | 9–10 hrs)

• Scenic drive via Almora & Thal.
• En route stop at Birthi Waterfall.
• Arrival in Munsiyari (known as ‘Little Kashmir’), check-in with Panchachuli views.
• Overnight stay in Munsiyari.


4️⃣ Day 4 – Munsiyari Sightseeing

• Witness sunrise over Panchachuli Peaks.
• Explore Khaliya Top trek (easy day hike).
• Visit Darkot Village (hand-woven pashmina shawls).
• Explore local culture & markets.
• Overnight in Munsiyari.


5️⃣ Day 5 – Drive to Chaukori (Munsiyari → Chaukori | 6 hrs)

• A picturesque journey through pine & rhododendron forests.
• Arrival at Chaukori, known for sunrise & sunset over snow peaks.
• Visit tea gardens & local temples.
• Overnight in Chaukori.


6️⃣ Day 6 – Chaukori → Kausani (5–6 hrs)

• Morning tea amidst Himalayan views.
• Drive to Kausani (called the ‘Switzerland of India’ by Mahatma Gandhi).
• Visit Anasakti Ashram (where Gandhi stayed), Rudradhari Falls & Caves.
• Overnight stay in Kausani.


6️⃣ Day 7 – Kausani → Ranikhet (3–4 hrs)

• Morning sunrise at Baijnath Temple complex.
• Drive to Ranikhet, famed for its orchards & colonial charm.
• Visit Chaubatia Gardens, Jhula Devi Temple, & Army Golf Course.
• Evening leisure walk in pine forests.
• Overnight in Ranikhet.

6️⃣ Day 8 – Ranikhet → Delhi/Kathgodam (7–8 hrs)

• Return journey with packed memories.
• Drop at Delhi/Kathgodam.

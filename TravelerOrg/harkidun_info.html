Har Ki Dun Treak Details

Har Ki Dun Trek

Har Ki Dun Trek – A Timeless Journey Into the Valley of Gods

“Where the rivers hum ancient hymns and the trails whisper stories of the skies.”

🛤️ Getting There

Your journey begins the moment you arrive in Dehradun, the capital of Uttarakhand. We provide comfortable pickup and drop services from:

• Jolly Grant Airport • Dehradun Railway Station

Let the hills welcome you as you travel through winding pine-laced roads to the charming hamlet of Sankri, the gateway to Har Ki Dun.

📆 6-Days Trek Itinerary (Sankri to Sankri)


1️⃣ Day 1 – Arrival at Sankri

• Arrival in Sankri by evening
• Check into scenic homestay
• Enjoy a warm welcome drink
• Orientation and trek briefing by the guide
• Dinner and overnight stay under star-lit Himalayan sky

2️⃣ Day 2 – Sankri to Taluka (Drive) | Taluka to Osla/Seema
(Trek Begins)

• Drive: 12 km to Taluka
• Trek: 10–12 km through the Govind Wildlife Sanctuary
• Spot wild orchids and the vibrant Tons River keeping you company
• Reach Osla/Seema campsite by afternoon
• Tents set, hot meals served
• Overnight in tents under the whispering pines


3️⃣ Day 3 – Osla/Seema to Har Ki Dun Valley

• Trek: 12 km ascending trail
• Cross wooden bridges, roaring streams, and silence-laced woods
• Reach the majestic Har Ki Dun Valley
• Surrounded by snow-capped peaks including the Swargarohini
• Evening tea, hot dinner, and tales by the bonfire
• Overnight camping at Har Ki Dun


4️⃣ Day 4 – Day Trek to Maninda Tal and Back

• Trek to the mystical Maninda Tal, a glacial alpine lake
• Meditate in the stillness, feel the divine presence
• Return to Har Ki Dun Valley campsite
• Leisure evening and photography
• Overnight stay at Har Ki Dun


5️⃣ Day 5 – Har Ki Dun to Osla/Seema (Descent)

• Trek back via the same scenic trail
• The mountains bid a gentle farewell
• Camp at Osla/Seema, with folk songs and laughter • Overnight stay in tents


6️⃣ Day 6 – Osla/Seema to Sankri (Descent)

Trek back to Sankri via Govind Sanctuary and the beautiful bridge over Tons River.
•Stay at Sankri Homestay with friends and our guides with bagful of stories. This is your time to arrange your memories and collect to your home.

✅ Do’s

  • Carry personal reusable water bottles
  • Respect local traditions and village culture
  • Keep your waste pouch with you always & Pack light but right
  • Listen to your guide’s instructions

🚫 Don’ts

  • No loud music or disruptive behavior
  • Don’t wander away alone from the group
  • Strictly no alcohol or drugs during the trek & Avoid littering or using plastic
  • Don’t harm flora/fauna or pluck rare plants
